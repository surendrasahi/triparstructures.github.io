Mohan Motupalli

Structural Engineer & Founder

In 2025, I founded Tripar Structures Pvt. Ltd., driven by a vision to deliver innovative, reliable, and efficient structural solutions. Leveraging over a decade of experience in the construction and engineering industry, I focus on integrating advanced design methodologies with practical execution to create sustainable and high-performing structures. At Tripar, I lead projects that combine precision, creativity, and engineering excellence to meet evolving industry demands.

As a structural engineer, I bring extensive expertise in design, analysis, and project execution. I hold an M.Tech in Structural Engineering and have successfully delivered complex structural projects across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors. My career is defined by a balance of technical depth and on-site problem-solving, ensuring that each project achieves structural integrity, efficiency, and long-term value.

Before founding Tripar Structures, I refined my expertise through progressive roles—as a Partner at RE Associates (Feb 2023 – Feb 2025), where I led structural design initiatives and execution strategies; as a Senior Design Engineer at Sri Harsha Consulting Engineers Pvt. Ltd. (Jan 2016 – Jan 2021); and during my early career at Green Brook Engineering Services and SLN Constructions, where I built a strong foundation in structural steel detailing and site execution. These experiences shaped my holistic approach to engineering—grounded in technical excellence, collaboration, and innovation.
